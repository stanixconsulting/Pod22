What we print

Every job is set, locked, and pulled by hand — the runs stay small on purpose.

Wedding & event stationery

Invitations, place cards, and programs, printed on cotton stock with a deep, tactile impression.

Business cards & letterhead

Everyday paper goods for people who want their correspondence to feel considered.

Small-run art prints

Limited editions of 25 to 100, hand-numbered, from wood and metal type in our own case.

How a print gets made

The same four steps, whether it's one card or a thousand.

01

Set the type

Letters are pulled from the case and composed by hand, line by line.

02

Lock the chase

The set type is wedged tight into a steel frame so nothing shifts under pressure.

03

Ink and pull a proof

We mix ink to match, then check the first sheet for depth and registration.

04

Run the edition

Once the proof is approved, we run the full count, sheet by sheet.
