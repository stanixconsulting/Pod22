A small shop, an old press.

Fieldnote Press started in a garage in 2015, when our founder bought a broken proofing press at an estate sale and spent a winter rebuilding it on a kitchen table. Ten years later it's still a two-person shop — we just got better at fixing presses.

The presses we run

Both older than either of us, both still doing the job they were built for.

Vandercook SP-15
1962 · cylinder proof press · our workhorse for cards and small posters.
Chandler & Price Pilot
1901 · hand-fed platen · used for short runs and anything foil-stamped.
California job case
Early 1900s · wood and metal type · the source of every letter we set by hand.

What we hold onto

Three habits that haven't changed since the garage days.

We set every line by hand

No digital plates. If a line looks slightly uneven, that's the type talking, not a mistake.

We keep runs small

We turn down jobs that need thousands of copies — that's not what these presses are for.

We show our proofs

You see and approve a physical proof before we run the full edition, every time.
